About Marrow Hill

Marrow Hill is a small UK business. We make plain-English guides and simple tools for people who sell on Vinted, eBay, Etsy, Gumroad and similar, and want to understand where they stand with HMRC without wading through jargon.

What we are, and are not

We are not accountants, tax advisers or HMRC. Our guides follow published GOV.UK guidance and we link to it so you can check for yourself. The free tax checker gives an estimate only. For anything complicated, speak to a qualified accountant.

What we sell

Our own Excel tools and guides (see the shop), plus a few 3D printed and handmade FPV drone accessories on Etsy. Everything is made and posted from the UK.

Your privacy
