A compact case that holds the Alien 3000mAh 2S LiPo and slots into the standard battery loop on Fat Shark style FPV goggles. It looks like the standard two-cell case, but it holds the flat LiPo pack instead.

How it fits together

The bottom lid clips on (a drop of glue is optional). The top lid hooks in and is held by one M3 x 6 mm countersunk hex screw. Body size is 86.8 x 45.1 x 20.9 mm.

Check before you buy

It is designed around the Alien 3000mAh 2S LiPo only, so check your battery fits. The battery is not included. The case holds the battery but is not fireproof, and you should not charge a battery inside it.
