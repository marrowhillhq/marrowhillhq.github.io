BetaFPV USB Cable for Betaflight

Lost or broken the little USB dongle for your BetaFPV whoop? This handmade cable connects the flight controller to your laptop or PC so you can get back to configuring in Betaflight.

SH1.0 4-pin USB cable + USB-A to USB-C adapter (£9.99, free UK postage)

Right-angle USB-A plug, black, about 17 inches (43 cm). Checked with a BetaFPV Air75 and Betaflight before posting. The drone is not included.

Buy on Etsy

Will it fit my drone?

It suits BetaFPV flight controllers that use the SH1.0 4-pin USB connection, including the Air75 and Air65 and other Tiny Whoop boards with that connector. Check the port on your board before ordering.

Using it

You must use the included adapter: the cable will not connect USB-C straight into a computer. Plug the SH1.0 end into the flight controller, connect to your computer, then connect in Betaflight Configurator as normal.
