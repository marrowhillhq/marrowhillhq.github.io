Do I pay tax on Vinted, eBay and Etsy sales?

Most people clearing out their wardrobe pay no tax. Things change if selling becomes a business.

Usually not taxable

  • Selling your own used clothes, furniture or gadgets for less than you paid.

Usually counts as trading

  • Buying items specifically to resell, such as from car boots, charity shops or wholesalers.
  • Making things to sell, such as crafts, prints or digital downloads.
  • Selling regularly with the aim of making a profit.

What to do if you are trading

  1. Keep a record of every sale and every cost, with dates.
  2. If total income is under £1,000 for the tax year, it is generally covered by the trading allowance.
  3. If it is over, register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year.

Records to keep

Sale date, platform, amount received, fees, postage and the cost of the item or materials. A simple spreadsheet is enough.
